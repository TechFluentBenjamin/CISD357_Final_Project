      Research Question 2 (RQ2): Is there a correlation between the number of people attending the game in person and the points scored by the home team?

      Hypothesis 2 (H2): The team believes there is a weak correlation between these two data points. There are multiple factors that may influence the points scored by the home NFL team, including wind speed, elevation, and player availability due to injury.
